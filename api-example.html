SKU Placements resource

Use the SKU placements resource to view, create, update and delete the placement of products in one of your stores. A placement maps a SKU to a pick location.

CONCEPTS

Understanding SKU placements

To enable a product to be picked in one of your stores, the store-picking application displays the required SKU to the picker along with its expected location. This helps direct pickers to the correct location and supports an efficient pick walk around the store.

This information — where the SKU is expected to be — is called a SKU placement. It is a mapping between a SKU and a pick location.

Finalization times: Updates made after a shipment's finalization time will not be reflected in that shipment. For example, if Shipment A finalizes at midnight and Shipment B at 15:00, a location update made at 10:00 will affect Shipment B but not Shipment A.

See the API guidance for configuring In-Store Fulfilment APIs for more information.
