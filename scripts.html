EDUCATION VIDEO

Chromebook Spin 11 storyboard

A short scripted dialogue showing a teacher and child describing Chromebook use in school.

Expected duration

174 seconds · Approx. 435 words at 2.5 words per second

Child

I used to use pens, paper and books to do my school work. But now I use this great laptop.

Teacher

Kids are always losing and damaging their school pens and books, so we decided to replace them with Chromebooks.

Child

In the mornings before lessons I can watch related videos and then use the camera to take pictures of important things.

Teacher

Keeping the kids engaged is important and, in tent mode, they can watch videos and take pictures useful to their projects.

Child

I like that I can make notes directly on the screen and then come back and look for them later. The pen that it comes with feels just like a real pencil.
